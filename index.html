Our Story

More Than a Trust —
A Movement of Grace

Elvar Trust is a registered charitable trust based in Basavanagudi, Bangalore. While our formal journey began in 2026, the spirit behind it has been alive for years — in quiet meals shared, in school supplies given, in medical bills quietly settled for those who couldn't afford it.

We don't wait for large budgets or perfect conditions. We serve now, with what we have, together with a community of friends, family, and colleagues who believe that generosity is contagious.

Our four pillars — Education, Food, Healthcare, and Mental Health — represent the most fundamental needs of the most vulnerable people around us. Every initiative we run flows from a simple conviction: every person deserves dignity.

🌱 Informally Serving Since 2022 · Formally Est. 2026

Rooted in Compassion

Born from a conviction that every person deserves dignity — access to learning, nourishment, healing, and hope. Long before formal registration, our founders were already quietly serving — through personal funds, through colleagues who believed in the same cause.

We believe giving is not an act but a way of life.

What We Do

Four Pillars of Service

Every initiative we run is anchored in one of four critical areas of human need.

📚

Education

Supporting children and young people with school supplies, scholarships, and tuition support — because every mind deserves a chance to grow.

🌾

Food

Running community feeding programs and distributing groceries — ensuring no one in our reach goes to bed hungry. Dignity begins at the table.

🏥

Healthcare

Facilitating access to basic medical care, medicines, and health checkups for underserved families — because health is a right, not a privilege.

🕊️

Mental Health

Providing counselling referrals, awareness programs, and compassionate listening — breaking the stigma one conversation at a time.
